## The words

| Term | Plain meaning | Changes when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Balance** | Deposits, withdrawals and closed trades. Ignores open ones. | A trade closes, or you move money |
| **Floating P/L** | Profit or loss on open trades. Not yours yet. | Every tick |
| **Equity** | Balance plus floating P/L. The live value. | Every tick |
| **Required margin** | The deposit for one trade | You open or close that trade |
| **Used margin** | All required margins added up | You open or close anything |
| **Free margin** | Equity minus used margin. Your cushion. | Every tick, and on every open or close |
| **Margin level** | Equity as a % of used margin | Every tick |
| **Margin call level** | Where the broker warns you. Often 100%. | Set by the broker |
| **Stop out level** | Where the broker closes you. Often 50%. | Set by the broker |

## The one-sentence version of each lesson

- **Margin** is a deposit, not a fee, and you get it back.
- **Balance** lies to you, because it ignores open trades.
- **Equity** is the truth.
- **Floating profit** is not money until you close.
- **Used margin** is money that cannot protect you.
- **Free margin** is your actual cushion.
- **Margin level** is what the broker judges you by.
- **Margin call** is the warning.
- **Stop out** is not.
- **Leverage** is the ceiling; **effective leverage** is your choice.
