## One honest caveat about spot

Retail spot forex has a catch worth knowing early: **your broker is often the other side of your trade.** You are not usually plugged into some grand global exchange. You are trading with a company that quotes you a price.

That is not automatically sinister, and regulated brokers operate under real rules. But it explains a lot of things you will meet later: why two brokers show slightly different prices, why spreads change, and why choosing a properly regulated broker matters more than almost any other decision you will make.

Kindergarten covers exactly that. For now, know that the door you are walking through is the spot market, and know who is standing on the other side of it.
