Open a trade and a number starts moving on your screen. Green, red, green again. That number is your **unrealised**, or **floating**, profit and loss.

<Formula expression="Floating P/L = (Current price − Entry price) × Position size" note="Reversed, naturally, if you are short." />

## It is a forecast, not a receipt

<Analogy>
It is half time and your team is 2-0 up. Are you celebrating?

Slightly, but nobody is putting it in the record books, because the game is still on. A 2-0 lead at half time is genuinely valuable information and it is not a result.

Floating profit is a half-time score.
</Analogy>

The number is real in the sense that you could close right now and take it. It is not real in the sense that you have it.

## Why it feels like money

Because your brain files it as money the moment it turns green. This produces two classic failures, and almost every trader commits both.

<TwoUp
  left={{
    title: 'Cutting winners early',
    tone: 'plain',
    points: [
      'The trade is plus $80 and you feel rich',
      'It dips to plus $60 and you panic',
      'You close it to protect the "profit"',
      'It goes on to plus $400 without you',
    ],
  }}
  right={{
    title: 'Letting losers run',
    tone: 'bad',
    points: [
      'The trade is minus $80 and it feels unreal',
      'Closing would make the loss actually happen',
      'So you wait for it to come back',
      'At minus $600 you are no longer trading, you are hoping',
    ],
  }}
/>

<Callout kind="warning" title="Where the asymmetry comes from">
A floating profit feels like something you might lose. A floating loss feels like something that has not happened yet.

Both feelings are wrong, and they push you in exactly the wrong direction: out of good trades early and into bad trades forever. Recognising the feeling is most of the cure.
</Callout>

## Where it goes on your platform

Floating P/L is what turns balance into **equity**:

<Formula expression="Equity = Balance + Floating P/L" />

That is the next lesson, and it is the number that actually decides whether your account survives the afternoon.
