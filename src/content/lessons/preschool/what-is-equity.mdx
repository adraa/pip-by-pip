## Balance versus equity

<Worked title="The same account, described two ways">
You deposited **$2,000**. You have two trades open, showing **minus $400** in total.

**Balance: $2,000.** Nothing has closed, so nothing has changed.

**Equity: $1,600.** That is what you would walk away with if you closed both trades this second.

Which number is the truth about your account?
</Worked>

Equity, obviously. Balance is a historical record. Equity is now.

<Callout kind="warning" title="This is why people are surprised">
A trader watching the balance sees $2,000 and feels fine. The broker is watching equity, sees $1,600, and is already counting down to closing the positions.

Every margin call calculation in the next four lessons uses equity. Balance never appears in any of them.
</Callout>

## Watch it breathe

Equity moves every second the market moves, because floating P/L is inside it. Drag the price slider and watch equity respond while balance sits perfectly still.

## The three-second sanity check

Before you close your laptop for the night, ask:

1. What is my equity?
2. What is my used margin?
3. How far can the market move before those two collide?

If you cannot answer the third, your position is too big. That collision has a name, **margin level**, and it is the next lesson.
