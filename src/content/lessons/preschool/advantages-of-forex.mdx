## And the fake one

Every broker advertises high leverage as a headline feature. It is not a feature. It is the mechanism by which most accounts are destroyed.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Leverage is a hazard with good marketing">
"Trade up to 500:1!" means "you can lose everything five hundred times faster". Nothing about high leverage helps a beginner. Regulators in Europe, the UK and Australia capped retail leverage at 30:1 precisely because of what it does to ordinary people.

If a broker's homepage leads with leverage rather than regulation, that tells you what they are selling.
</Callout>

Nobody is going to tell you that forex is unusually forgiving, because it is not. It is unusually **accessible**, which is a different thing, and it is why this is where almost everyone begins.
